Home / Why Us

The digitizer stays the same. So does the quality.

Most digitizing shops rotate your file between whoever's free. We assign one digitizer to your account so every order comes back consistent — same density, same underlay logic, same fit.

01

Fair, upfront pricing

No per-color surcharges hidden until checkout, no "rush fee" sprung on you at hour six. What you're quoted is what you pay, whether the file is a two-color left chest or a fifteen-thousand-stitch jacket back.

02

Fastest bench-to-download time

Standard files move in 6–8 hours. That's not a marketing number — it's the same queue your order lands in during our busiest weeks, because we staff for peak volume instead of average volume.

03

A free trial that's actually free

Your first file — full quality, full revisions — costs nothing. We'd rather you judge our stitch-out on your own machine than take our word for it.

04

One digitizer, one account manager

You get a named person who learns your fabric, your machine, and your preferences — not a new contractor every order. Revisions go straight to someone who already knows the file.

05

Quality assurance before it ever reaches you

Every file is run through a manual QA pass — stitch density, pull compensation, underlay — before it's released, so what lands in your inbox is machine-ready, not a first draft.
